How to read this page

In the flow tab, a solid residual arrow increases an input edge's flow; a dashed orange arrow cancels it. Only positive residual capacities are drawn. Edge labels show residual capacity and the residual edge ID. Switch off residual view to see flow/capacity on the input edges. Every input edge owns an even forward ID and an odd reverse ID; edge ^ 1 swaps them. The current path is blue, and the inspected edge is orange.

Try Cancellation required. After one unit takes 0-1-3-5, the next route is 0-2-3-1-4-5. Its step 3-1 undoes the old flow on 1-3. In the matching tab, try A partner must move: L0 gives up R0 for R1, freeing R0 for L1. Partner arrays can temporarily disagree during an individual path-flip assignment; an amber note marks these steps.

Previous and the history slider revisit snapshots. Next augmentation stops after a completed push or matching flip. Finish runs to the optimum. Changing the graph resets the execution. On narrow screens, scroll the diagram sideways to inspect it. Teaching limits keep diagrams readable: at most 10 flow vertices and 30 input edges, or 8 vertices per matching side and 32 pairs. Capacities are integers from 0 to 1000000000.
