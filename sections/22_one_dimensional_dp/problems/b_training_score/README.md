# B. Training Score

You have `n` training days. If you train on day `i`, you gain `a[i]` points.
You may not train on two consecutive days. You may also skip all days.

Find the maximum total score.

## Input

```text
n
a1 a2 ... an
```

`1 <= n <= 2 * 10^5`, `-10^9 <= ai <= 10^9`.

## Output

Print the maximum score.

## Sample

Input:

```text
6
5 1 2 10 6 2
```

Output:

```text
17
```
