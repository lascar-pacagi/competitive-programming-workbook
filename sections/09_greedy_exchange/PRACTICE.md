# Section 9 Practice Queue

Use this queue after finishing the local exercises and reading the editorial.
The goal is to practice recognizing the local choice and writing the exchange
argument before coding.

## Core

1. Codeforces 489B - BerSU Ball  
   https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/489/B  
   Focus: sorting, matching smallest sufficient partner, two pointers.

2. Kattis - Interval Scheduling  
   https://open.kattis.com/problems/intervalscheduling  
   Focus: earliest-finish interval scheduling under ICPC-style input.

3. AtCoder ABC091 / ARC092 A - 2D Plane 2N Points  
   https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc091/tasks/arc092_a  
   Focus: greedy matching after sorting one coordinate.

## Stretch

4. AtCoder ABC137 D - Summer Vacation  
   https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc137/tasks/abc137_d  
   Focus: heap greedy, choosing the best available job at each day.

## How To Review

For each accepted solution, write three sentences:

1. What is the greedy choice?
2. Why can an optimal solution be modified to make that choice?
3. What data structure makes the choice efficient?
