# Section 10 Practice Queue

These problems emphasize building an answer while preserving a prefix or suffix
invariant.

## Core

1. AtCoder ABC136 C - Build Stairs  
   https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc136/tasks/abc136_c  
   Focus: left-to-right feasibility after small local repairs.

2. Codeforces 1153C - Serval and Parenthesis Sequence  
   https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1153/C  
   Focus: bracket quotas, prefix balance, and impossibility.

3. Kattis - Planting Trees  
   https://open.kattis.com/problems/plantingtrees  
   Focus: ordering by the largest remaining bottleneck.

## Stretch

4. Kattis - Building Boundaries  
   https://open.kattis.com/problems/buildingboundaries  
   Focus: constructive case enumeration; useful ICPC-style discipline even
   though the greedy component is smaller.

## How To Review

After solving, write:

1. The invariant maintained after each step.
2. The earliest point where impossibility can be detected.
3. A counterexample to at least one tempting but wrong construction.
