# Section 12 Practice Queue

Use this queue after the local exercises. For each problem, identify the
candidate that becomes dominated and explain why it can be removed.

## Core

1. Codeforces 91B - Queue  
   https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/91/B  
   Focus: monotone structure plus binary/search-style reasoning over useful
   suffix information.

2. AtCoder ABC217 E - Sorting Queries  
   https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc217/tasks/abc217_e  
   Focus: queue behavior mixed with a priority queue.

3. Kattis - Even Up Solitaire  
   https://open.kattis.com/problems/evenup  
   Focus: stack cancellation and local removals.

## Stretch

4. LeetCode 862 - Shortest Subarray with Sum at Least K  
   https://leetcode.com/problems/shortest-subarray-with-sum-at-least-k/  
   Focus: monotonic deque over prefix sums, same core idea as local problem C.

## How To Review

For each solution, write:

1. What does the container store?
2. What condition removes candidates?
3. Why is every element removed at most once?
